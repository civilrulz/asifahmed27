During my Geotechnical research, I had the opportunity to work on several projects focused on highway slope stability, pavement distress development, real-time data analysis, and finite element numerical modeling. In recent years, my work has expanded toward climate-informed hazard assessment and the use of advanced data analytics in geotechnical applications. I have led projects that integrate remote sensing data, mobile applications, and AI-driven platforms to develop scalable tools for hazard monitoring and prediction.
