Research

Research Overview

My research focuses on geotechnical engineering, infrastructure resilience, and the integration of emerging technologies such as remote sensing, artificial intelligence, and data analytics into civil engineering applications. My work aims to improve the monitoring, prediction, and management of infrastructure systems affected by environmental and climatic hazards.

Research Goal

My research experience can be attributed to Geotechnical and Geoenvironmental Engineering which can be summarized as: (1) Performance Monitoring of Geo-infrastructure through Field Instrumentation and FEM Analysis, (2) Geo-physical Testing for Evaluation of Geohazard Potential and Infrastructure Health Monitoring, (3) Laboratory Testing (Both Saturated and Unsaturated) of GeoMaterials, and (4) Non Destructive Testing of Alternative Recycled Materials for Pavement.

During my Geotechnical research, I had the opportunity to work on several projects focused on highway slope stability, pavement distress, subgrade moisture modeling, recycled materials (RAP) testing, geo-physical testing on subbase, use of geosynthetics in pavement moisture control. I was also involved in Bio-reactor Landfills to evaluate the moisture variation and efficiency of leachate recirculation in the Geoenvironmental sector.

My ultimate focus is to maximize the lifespan of the transportation infrastructure through proper investigation, providing cost effective and sustainable solutions.

Research Highlights

My research focuses on geotechnical engineering and infrastructure resilience, with strong interest in instrumentation, modeling, geospatial data, and practical solutions that improve transportation systems.

Pavement Freeze–Thaw Analysis

Freezing and thawing of subgrade soils can significantly damage transportation infrastructure in cold regions. This work identifies the timing of freezing and thawing using real-time monitoring data.

Recycled Plastic Slope Stabilization

Recycled Plastic Pins (RPPs) offer a cost-effective solution for improving highway slope stability, providing a sustainable alternative to conventional stabilization methods.

Subgrade Instrumentation

Pavement instrumentation enables continuous observation of subsurface behavior, capturing real-time changes in moisture and temperature for improved performance evaluation.

Geosynthetic Crack Mitigation

A combined geotextile–geomembrane–geonet moisture barrier system has been used to reduce flexible pavement edge cracking and improve long-term durability.

Geo-Infrastructure FEM Modeling

Finite Element Modeling (FEM) is used to evaluate highways, pavements, dams, and tunnels under varying loading and environmental conditions to better predict performance.

Geophysical Failure Investigation

Resistivity imaging provides continuous subsurface profiles and is an efficient tool for forensic investigation of slope failures and seepage issues in earth structures.

Recycled Pavement Materials

Due to the depletion of virgin aggregates, recycled materials such as RAP and RCCA are increasingly used in pavement base layers to support sustainable transportation infrastructure.
