Welcome

A bit about me:

I am an Assistant Professor at the State University of New York Polytechnic Institute (SUNY Poly), where my work focuses on geotechnical engineering and the integration of emerging technologies into civil engineering practice and education. My research lies at the intersection of geotechnical engineering, remote sensing, artificial intelligence, and engineering education, with an emphasis on developing innovative tools to better understand and predict infrastructure and environmental hazards.

I received my Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) and later earned my Ph.D. in Geotechnical Engineering from the University of Texas at Arlington under the supervision of Dr. Sahadat Hossain. My research experience spans unsaturated soil mechanics, expansive soil behavior, slope stability, pavement distress development, real-time data analysis, and finite element numerical modeling.

In recent years, my work has expanded toward climate-informed hazard assessment and the use of advanced data analytics in geotechnical applications. I have led projects that integrate remote sensing data, mobile applications, and AI-driven platforms to develop scalable tools for hazard monitoring and prediction.

As an educator, I am committed to developing innovative curricula that integrate sustainability, equity, and emerging technologies into civil engineering education. I actively mentor both undergraduate and graduate students and enjoy guiding them as they explore research, develop technical skills, and prepare for professional careers.

Beyond my academic work, I contribute to the engineering profession through leadership and service roles in organizations such as the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) and ABET. I am also involved in outreach efforts with local schools, community colleges, and professional societies to promote engineering education and broaden participation in STEM fields.

I am a licensed Professional Engineer (P.E.) in the state of New York.
